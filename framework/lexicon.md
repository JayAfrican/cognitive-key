## 5 · Publishing rules (the book chain) · FILED, review deferred by your ruling (C3)

1. A narrative beat doesn't generate its contents.
2. An emotional state doesn't generate its contents.
3. A gap in a source known to be partial is logged as a gap, never as a result.
4. **A document is edited in its source and published from there, never at the point of publication.** This repository is now the source.
5. **A caveat is a claim:** name the objection a hedge is aimed at, and check it's the live one.
6. **An edit must preserve the function of the thing it edits.**

*Rules 1–3 say the shape of a container never generates its contents. Rule 4 says a container never becomes the source.*
