# The Framework

**Canonical file 1 of the framework (code phase 3). Built 2026-11-17 from the rulings only; nothing here is new.** Each section says who settled it.

**RULED 2026-11-17 as a whole.** JayArr read it and chose *"Yes, rule it"*. This is now the master statement of what the Framework is, and older documents defer to it. The tools were ruled the same day, in their own files.

---

## In one sentence

A way of taking something apart (a sentence about a person, a claim, a system, a word) to see what it's really made of, and then putting it back together honestly.

## The cycle · RULED 2026-10-11

**Absorb → deconstruct → reconstruct.**

| Step | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Absorb** | Take the thing in as it is |
| **Deconstruct** | Ask the five questions |
| **Reconstruct** | Rewrite it so every claim names its unit and its ruler, or say it can't be rewritten honestly with what's there *(rewrite step: RULED 2026-10-03)* |

## The five questions · RULED 2026-09-25

| # | The question |
|---|---|
| 1 | **Name the unit.** What is this a property of: a signal, a cell, a text, a person, a moment, an audience? |
| 2 | **Where is the ruler?** Measured against what, by whom, in what order, and was the comparison stated? |
| 3 | **How many things are there, and can you tell from inside?** |
| 4 | **What does the step cost?** |
| 5 | **Who holds the pen, and who pays for the error?** |

**What testing shows so far · CORROBORATED (Campaign E, two blind runs on two models):** question 2 is the one capable readers skip, so it carries most of the value. **Blind, 2026-11-20:** with the rewrite step added, the rewrite came back 3 of 3; without it, 0 of 6. **Still untested on a person:** that's the Two Sentences test.

## The design rule: parallel lenses · RULED 2026-10-07

> *"Your best state is fusion: the whole arrives at once. Your design rule is parallel lenses, because a whole that arrives at once can't be checked from inside, so the check is built outside, in separate pieces. The rule isn't in tension with the state; it's the brake sized to it."*

## What's in it, and what isn't · RULED 2026-10-03

| Layer | What it is | Part of the Framework? |
|---|---|---|
| **Framework** | Takes information apart: the cycle, the questions, the tools | **Yes** |
| **Tester** | Checks whether the Framework works: predictions, blind runs, scores | No |
| **Plumbing** | Voice notes, the Ledger, screens | No |

**And the Octave isn't the Framework (RULED 2026-10-11).** The Octave maps *you*, the person running the Framework. Keeping them apart keeps the check outside.

## The tools · RULED 2026-11-17 as his tools (steps open to correction by tests)

The specialised instruments for the deconstruct step. Each has its own canonical file in `tools/`.

| Tool | For | Test record |
|---|---|---|
| **Stopping Engine** | A claim ("studies show…") | Blind-tested: Campaigns B, B retest, C |
| **Cross-pollination intake** | A pair said to be opposites, or the same | Blind-tested: Campaign A, 18 runs |
| **Recoherence** | One word with many senses | 5 runs + 2 blind (2026-11-20): refused a false theme, and refused a real family |
| **Sounding** | A whole system | 3 runs + 1 blind on another model: LOAD-BEARING on the null case |
| **Jargon Breaker** | Language that won't say who did what to whom | 3 runs + 1 blind: 4 of 4 verdicts right |
| **Axis Search** | Two unrelated things: is there one dimension under both? | 1 run + 2 blind: refused a nonsense pair; rerun pending on a real one |

## What would show it's useless · from §0.7, RULED 2026-09-25

The frame is made of questions, not claims, so it can't be proved wrong, only shown to be useless. **The test:** *does asking these five give a person anything they wouldn't get without them?*
