## 3 · The eight notes · his structure, from *The Loop*

A closed loop in four phases. Every note has its own way of getting stuck, and **naming the note you're stuck at is the first move in getting unstuck.**

| Phase | Note | What it is | How it gets stuck |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Intake** | 1 · **Root** | Baseline: the floor everything else stands on | Unsettled; everything after it costs more |
| | 2 · **Spark** | Ignition: something enters and the cycle wakes | Ignition with nowhere to land: tabs, starts, no finish |
| **Compression** | 3 · **Polarity** | The gut read: toward or away, safe or not, before thought | **"Can't stop feeling it."** The read fires and locks |
| | 4 · **Friction** | Two things that don't reconcile; load builds fastest | **"Can't start doing it."** The answer is whole but won't become a first step |
| **Ejection** | 5 · **Execution** | Release: hyperfocus, flow, done | Output with no target |
| | 6 · **Shadow** | The comedown: clearing and consolidating | Mistaken for a crash and fought |
| **Integration** | 7 · **Surrender** | The deliberate stop: the kill switch | Can't stop; resting feels like failure |
| | 8 · **Gestalt** | It resolves into sense; the loop closes back to Root, changed | *(no stall named)* |

**The two stalls that need opposite fixes** (from *The Loop* and the public edition):
- **Stuck at the 3rd** needs a body-first intervention below it: breath, grounding, a long exhale. Not more analysis.
- **Stuck at the 4th** needs physical anchoring and something in the environment to ignite the 5th. Not more grounding.

**The three dials · MIXED** (the claim is his; the wording and literature are the mirror's). Everyone runs the same loop at different settings:
- how much gets in (filter width)
- what it costs to stop and switch (switch cost)
- how long recovery takes (recovery time)

## 6 · The law of the two poles · **RULED 2026-11-17, untested**

Your older documents give each note **two poles**, a degradation and a transmutation (the Transmutation Protocol, the Shadow Framework's paired poles). Your newest findings explain **how you move between them**:

- **63:** survival adaptations keep their skill and change their purpose, from protection to choice.
- **64:** chosen versions revert under dysregulation, so whether something is a "trait" depends on your state, gated by a regulation threshold.
- **65:** practice lowers the threshold, so chosen versions show up more often.

Put together: **each note's degradation and transmutation poles are the same skill, pointed at protection or at choice. Regulation decides which one shows up, and practice moves the line.** The examples are already in your findings:
- hypervigilance → the calibrated sensor (28)
- shutdown → chosen cutoff (34, 37)
- dissociation → Transmutive Consociation (47)
- numbing → chosen indifference (31)

**Ruled as a law of the Octave** (JayArr: *"Yes, that's the law"*). **Ruled doesn't mean tested.** The falsifier: a skill still in survival mode that never shifts with regulation, or a transmutation pole that shows up with no regulation behind it. It's the first Octave claim that needs a test plan.
