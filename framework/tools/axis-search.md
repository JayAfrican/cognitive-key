# Axis Search

**The generative half of the apparatus, built with the brake already attached.** Hand it A and B with **no claimed relation**. It asks whether a single dimension exists on which both sit. The output is never "A resembles B". It's **"A and B are the same variable at different settings."** The formal name for the move is projection onto a latent dimension, the qualitative cousin of factor analysis.

**Test record: UNTESTED.** Its generative form has never been run. Both worked examples (precision weighting; a fixed processing budget) were reached by testing a claimed opposition, not by this search. **Label every result: generated, unconfirmed.** It's a candidate until something independent scores it.

## Refuse at intake when it's the wrong instrument

- **A claimed opposition** ("A vs B") → route to **cross-pollination-intake**.
- **A membership claim** ("A is an instance of B") → not an axis question. C1 can't be written because the relation is kind, not degree. Record the refusal as the result.
- **A pair JayArr has already paired** → fine, but say so; C6's novelty bar is higher.

## The six constraints: no result until all six pass, stop at the first failure

**C1 · Positions, not categories.** Write: "A is high on X, B is low on X." If it can't be written without hedging, there's no axis, only a resemblance. **Stop.**

**C2 · The axis existed before you needed it.** It must be independently named, and ideally measured, in some literature **before** this pairing. **An axis coined to join A and B describes the pairing, not the world.** This is the hardest constraint. *(r/K selection died on C2; fast–slow life history survived because its axis can be measured without anyone's pairing.)*

**C3 · Exclusion at axis strength.** Three things high on the axis that resemble A in nothing; three low that resemble B in nothing. If only A and B are on it, it's a line through two points, which is always possible and never informative.

**C4 · Direction.** Same end or opposite? **Opposite is stronger:** it predicts an anticorrelation someone can go and look for. Same end is usually a shared cause and often trivial.

**C5 · Third domain.** Name a domain containing neither A nor B where the axis is measurable, and say how it behaves there. *(If ten honest attempts pass nothing, C5 is the one to loosen first; it bears the least weight.)*

**C6 · New prediction.** The axis must predict something about A or B that wasn't known before pairing them. No new prediction means the axis restates the pairing. **Stop, and log the attempt.**

## Warning: what an unconstrained axis becomes

"One dimension explains everything" is exactly the shape a bad actor needs (r/K was picked up by race pseudoscience after ecology dropped it). **An axis about people needs C2 at full strength and gets no benefit of the doubt.**
