# Cross-Pollination Intake

The standing procedure for testing a new abstract theory, a claimed correspondence between domains, an analogy, or an asserted opposition. Run it top to bottom. Stop where it says stop.

**Trigger this whenever any of these appear:** "X maps onto Y", "I noticed a connection between…", "isn't this the same as…", "the octave applies to…", a claimed pattern across domains, an asserted binary ("is it A or B?"), or a request to test whether an analogy is real.

**One rule above the others: complete the sheet even when the verdict is obvious.** The steps that feel unnecessary on an obvious pass are the ones that catch the non-obvious failure. A log containing only interesting entries is a log that has been curated.

**Report the sheet as you run it** — sections, findings, verdict. Do not present a conclusion without the steps that produced it.

---

## 0 · Intake

Capture before analysing:

- **The anomaly** — what actually caught attention, stated *before* any domain is named.
- **Candidate domain(s).**
- **What you expect to find** — write it down now, so the sheet can contradict you. If it does, say so explicitly and discard the prediction.
- **Where it arrived from** — a conversation, a reading, a memory, an image.

> **Stop condition.** If the anomaly cannot be stated without naming the framework it is supposed to support, this is confirmation shopping, not testing. Say so and stop.

---

## 1 · Level check *(structure mapping — Gentner 1983)*

Decompose before comparing. State the correspondence at each level and mark where it actually lives.

| Level | What it is | Holds? |
|---|---|---|
| **Title** | the label, the shared token, the name | |
| **Plot** | the particulars, events, mechanisms | |
| **Theme** | the underlying relational structure | |

A shared word is a fact about language, not a shared concept. A shared mechanism is a real correspondence. A shared relational structure is the only level at which a mapping can be transferable.

**The exclusion test — run it, or the theme is doing no work:**

- Name three things sharing the **surface** but not the theme.
- Name three things sharing the **theme** but not the surface.

> **Stop condition.** Cannot do both? The theme is too abstract. *The higher the abstraction, the cheaper the match.* Record as a title-level or plot-level coincidence and stop.

**Similarity is not transitive** (Tversky 1977). A resembles B and B resembles C licenses nothing about A and C. Ancestry *is* transitive — which is what §2 checks.

---

## 2 · Admissibility

**A1 · Ancestry.** Does the candidate domain share a source with the framework being tested? Check these contaminated lines first:

> **Hermeticism · New Thought · tarot, astrology, numerology · NLP and hypnosis · hemispheric pop-neuroscience · Campbell/Vogler monomyth**

If a source is shared, **agreement is circular and carries no evidential weight.** Say so explicitly rather than hedging.

**A special case worth naming: a theme that fits perfectly and comes from the framework's own signature is the most dangerous kind of pass.** If the surviving theme is a restatement of what the method was built to find, strike it under A1 — do not hedge it.

**A2 · Design.** Was it built by people?

If yes, it is inadmissible as evidence for its own design intent — but **admissible under the convergence clause** where it and an independent system arrived at the same solution under the same constraint with no contact. State which of the two is being claimed.

---

## 3 · Fit tests — T1 to T4, stop at first failure

**Ladder check first.** Is the candidate a stacked hierarchy? **If yes, run T3 before T1** — hierarchies almost always break at closure, and their ordered appearance is what hides it.

| | Test |
|---|---|
| **T1** | Ordered phases where the order carries meaning — a process, not a taxonomy |
| **T2** | Can it seize *mid-process*, under load? **Strongest filter** |
| **T3** | Does it close and feed the start again at a changed level? |
| **T4** | Did the mapping make you look somewhere you would not otherwise have looked? |

**Before recording a failure, check the scale.** A domain can fail at the grain tried first and pass at another. *Am I looking at the right unit?*

Classify the failure: **clean fail · broken stage · count conflict · wrong scale.**

---

## 4 · Inversion check

### 4a · Residual-category screen — run this first

**Was the second term of the pair coined as the residual of the first?** Check etymology and coinage date, not current usage. Tells: a *non-*, *para-*, *a-*, *sub-*, *pre-* or *atypical* prefix; a coinage postdating its partner by decades or centuries; a definition statable only by reference to the other term.

**If yes, predict before going further:**

1. **Two dimensions rather than one axis.**
2. **The second term will fail to be one thing** — expect a grab-bag whose contents share nothing but the instrument that missed them.

*Worked instances:* EQ (Thorndike's "social intelligence", 1920, named as what the tests miss; Wechsler's "non-intellective factors") · parasympathetic (Langley, c. 1905, *beside* the sympathetic) · the "minor"/"non-dominant" hemisphere (post-Broca, a century of being defined by what the left one wasn't doing) · junk DNA · dark matter · prokaryote · invertebrate · every *NOS* / *atypical* / *subthreshold* diagnostic bin.

**Rescue clause.** A residual category can be repaired by an independent positive criterion — *parasympathetic* was named by subtraction and then defined anatomically by craniosacral outflow, and it is not a grab-bag. Check for such a criterion before predicting (2).

**Scope.** Empirical categories only. Formal residuals — the irrational numbers — are exhaustively specified by their definition and are perfectly coherent. If you find an **empirical** pair named as not-A that turns out to be a genuine natural kind, the screen is refuted; log it.

### 4b · The four questions

1. Can both be high at once? *(If yes → two dimensions, not one axis.)*
2. Do cases pile up in the middle? *(A proliferating* atypical / subthreshold / mixed *residual is the tell.)*
3. One mechanism at two settings, or two mechanisms?
4. Does the framing keep generating questions that never resolve?

Where data exists, **Meehl's taxometrics** (MAXCOV, MAMBAC) outranks all four.

**Verdict: true dichotomy / single axis / unresolved.** If single axis — name the axis, and say whether it is a candidate parameter.

**A verdict of "single axis" is not licence to discard the findings built on the dichotomy.** Sympathetic and parasympathetic remained real and useful after autonomic space replaced the bipolar model. *The correction changes the geometry, not the phenomena.*

---

## 5 · Rescue check *(only if something failed)*

**You may re-specify once.** Lakatos: a rescue must generate a prediction the original did not make.

- What failed, and at which level?
- The re-specified claim.
- **What new prediction does it make?**

> **Stop condition.** No new prediction → **degenerating**. The rescue only re-worded the claim. Log the original failure and stop.

**Establish what the claim actually was before rejecting it.** A claim may fail at plot level and hold at theme level. *"Dopamine mutates DNA"* is refuted; *"chemicals alter the genome"* is true and mutagens are a well-characterised class. Do not let the first take the second.

**Three fates, and pick the right one:** *retired* (superseded by a better-resolution account) · *shelved* (**observation sound, mechanism missing — the correct verdict when the thing was seen but not explained**) · *refuted* (the observation itself does not hold).

## 6 · Verdict and routing

| | |
|---|---|
| **Verdict** | pass · clean fail · broken stage · count conflict · wrong scale · single axis · true dichotomy |
| **Admissible as** | pattern mapping · inversion entry · concordance entry · lexicon term · illustration only · nothing |
| **What it changes** | |
| **What it does *not* license** | |
| **Trap to name** | the flattering reading this hands over — write it down so it is not written up |

**Routing:**

- Passes T1–T4 with A1/A2 clear → **pattern mapping**; write the rows before any prose.
- Fails T1/T2/T3 but the *theme* holds → not a mapping. Route to **concordance** or **illustration only**.
- Resolves an opposition → **inversion log**.
- Names a phenomenon that had no name → **lexicon**, with its *does not mean* boundary.
- Contradicts an existing framework claim → **concordance**, with a disposition.
- **Nothing survives → log the failure anyway.** The failure log is what makes the rest credible, and rejections are the part competitors structurally cannot copy.

---

## 7 · Register and consent gate

Before anything leaves in a shareable form:

- Does any claim rest on **first-person report**? Attach the **trained-instrument** caveat.
- Does any claim rest on **agreement with a contaminated source**? It is **struck, not hedged**.
- Does it involve a **third party who has not consented**? It does not leave in any form, **including anonymised**.
- Is it **clinically weighty**? Do not draft without asking.
- Does the short version **drop scope rather than caveats**? A short piece can honestly be one observation with no theory attached. It cannot honestly be the full claim with the hedges removed.
- **Origin is not evidence.** Biography explains why someone was looking. It supports no claim.

**Never appears in outward-facing output:** left brain / right brain and gestalt-vs-linear as a two-category claim · neurotypical/neurodivergent as two realities (use *a continuum of Umwelten*) · ego/soul (use *self/content defusion*) · thinkers/feelers · quantum mechanics in any explanatory role · physiognomy · NLP as a citation (individual interventions may survive on independent evidence; the theory never supports anything).

---

## The obligation when rejecting

**When you reject, you owe the neighbourhood.** A rejection delivered without the adjacent true claim is not reflection, it is erasure — it leaves the thinker with a smaller map than they started with. If you strike "dopamine changes DNA," you must supply mutagens, epigenetic modification, and transient expression change in the same breath. If you strike a hemispheric claim, say what *is* lateralised.

And constrain without substituting: a returned construct must remain recognisably the thinker's, or the mirror has replaced them rather than reflected them.

## Worked example — compressed form

> **Anomaly:** *autism* and *autochthon* share a root.
> **Levels:** Title — shared *autos*, rejected as a fact about Greek. Plot — 1911 clinical coinage vs Athenian civic myth, no correspondence. **Theme — a person whose organising pattern is not derived from the surrounding social body, and what the namer decides that is worth. Holds.**
> **Exclusion test:** *automobile*, *autograph* share surface not theme; the metic distinction and the double empathy problem share theme not surface. **Passes.**
> **A1:** no shared source. **A2:** both are human classifications — structure found is a fact about classification, which is what the claim is about.
> **T1/T2/T3:** all fail — no phases, no seizure, no return.
> **Inversion:** one operation, two directions — the social valuation of self-derivation. **Single axis.**
> **Verdict:** mapping rejected; theme admissible. Routes to essay and inversion log.
> **Does not license:** anything about who was here first.
> **Trap:** *"neurodivergent people are the autochthones."*
