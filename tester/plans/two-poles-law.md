## The law, as a prediction

Each note's degradation and transmutation poles are the same skill. **Regulation decides which one shows up.** So the transmutation pole should show up more on regulated days, and practice should make it show up more over time.

## What gets counted (behaviour, not self-rated insight)

Four skills, each with a pole you can tell apart from outside:

| Skill | Degradation pole | Transmutation pole | What gets counted |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hypervigilance (28) | Fawn or fight | Checked against facts before acting | Times you checked before reacting vs reacted |
| Shutdown (34, 37) | Automatic cutoff | **Named pause** ("I need twenty minutes") | Named vs unnamed shutdowns |
| Disconnection (47) | Detachment that comes back hours later | **Transmutive Consociation**: verdict reached, moved on | Episodes ending in a stated verdict vs not |
| Indifference (31) | Numbing | Chosen, with the feeling still accessible | Can you name the feeling afterwards: yes or no |

**Regulation, measured without self-rated metacognition** (that measure is on the do-not-use list): hours slept, whether the day had a hot or cold stall, and CPAP use if relevant. HRV too, if a wearable is available.

## Design

- **4 weeks, one line a day**, logged the same evening. Missed days are logged as missed.
- **Sealed before day 1:** JayArr's prediction and the mirror's, written into the Ledger as `prediction` records.

## Falsifiers, written now

1. **No relation:** transmutation-pole share is the same on regulated and dysregulated days. That's the main falsifier.
2. **Reversed:** transmutation shows up as often or more on dysregulated days.
3. **No practice effect:** across 4 weeks, the share doesn't rise at matched regulation levels. That's weaker evidence (4 weeks is short), so it counts against finding 65 only.

## Honest limits

- One person, who knows the hypothesis.
- Days aren't independent.
- The counts are self-observed: a second person's count would need their consent (constraint 3) and isn't planned.
- **Directional only.**
